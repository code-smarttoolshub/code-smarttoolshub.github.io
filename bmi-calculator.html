What Is Body Mass Index (BMI)?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a widely used screening measurement that estimates whether a person has a healthy body weight for their height. It is calculated by dividing a person's weight in kilograms by the square of their height in meters (kg/m²).

Standard Adult BMI Categories

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), adult BMI values fall into four main categories: Underweight (below 18.5), Normal or Healthy Weight (18.5 to 24.9), Overweight (25.0 to 29.9), and Obesity (30.0 or higher).

Limitations to Keep in Mind

While BMI is a helpful general indicator for most adults, it does not directly measure body fat percentage or distinguish between muscle mass and fat. Athletes with high muscle mass may have a high BMI despite having low body fat. Always consult a healthcare professional for personalized fitness or medical guidance.
