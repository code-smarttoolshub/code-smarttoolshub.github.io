What Is an EMI (Equated Monthly Installment)?

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are used to pay off both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

How Is Loan EMI Calculated?

Our free online EMI Calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1], where P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the loan tenure in months.

Benefits of Calculating EMI Before Borrowing

Knowing your exact monthly installment before applying for a home loan, car loan, or personal loan helps you plan your monthly budget, compare interest rates from different banks, and choose a comfortable repayment tenure without financial strain.
