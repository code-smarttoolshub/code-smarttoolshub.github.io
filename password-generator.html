Why Do You Need a Strong Random Password?

Using weak or repeated passwords across multiple websites is one of the biggest online security risks. Hackers use automated brute-force software capable of guessing simple words, birthdays, and common number combinations in seconds. A truly random password combining uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols drastically reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

How Our Free Password Generator Works

This tool generates passwords locally inside your web browser using JavaScript. None of the passwords you create are ever sent over the internet or stored on any server, ensuring 100% privacy and security for your email, banking, and social media accounts.

Best Practices for Password Security

Cybersecurity experts recommend using passwords that are at least 12 to 16 characters long, enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible, and never reusing the same password on two different platforms.
