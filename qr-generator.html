How Does a Free Online QR Code Generator Work?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores website links, plain text, contact details, or promotional offers that smartphones can scan in less than a second. Our free online QR Code Generator converts any URL or message into a crisp, scannable QR image without watermarks or expiration dates.

Popular Ways to Use Custom QR Codes

Businesses, creators, and event organizers use QR codes on restaurant menus, product packaging, business cards, flyers, and social media profiles to send users directly to a webpage without forcing them to type long URLs manually.

How to Create and Download Your QR Code

Simply paste your link or type your message into the box above, click "Generate QR Code," and press the download button to save the PNG image to your device. Always test your generated QR code with your phone camera before printing.
